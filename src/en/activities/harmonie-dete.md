Every summer since 2023, **Harmonie en été** opens its doors to children and teenagers aged 5 to 18 in the Delmas neighbourhood of Port-au-Prince. This artistic and sports camp is organized by **Seraphin Kids** — Notre Demain contributes by supporting the organization so that each edition can welcome as many young people as possible.

*Give your child the chance to explore their artistic potential!*

## Programme

- **Music workshop** — each child chooses their instrument and learns the basics
- **Art workshop** — free and guided drawing and painting
- **Singing and vocal technique workshop** — expression and self-confidence
- **Mime workshop** — non-verbal communication and physical creativity
- **Educational games** — playful learning and teamwork
- **Football tournament** — fair play and team spirit on the field
- **Final show** — children present their creations to their families

## In numbers

Launched in July 2023 with 40 children, the camp now welcomes **nearly 50 young people** each summer over four weeks — from July 30 to August 31.
